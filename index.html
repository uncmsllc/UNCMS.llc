Who We Are

Transforming Lives Through Compassionate Care

Unique Nursing Care Management Services, LLC (UNCMS, LLC) is a substance abuse recovery organization dedicated to transforming the lives of individuals struggling with drug and/or alcohol addiction. We believe recovery is possible for everyone.

We treat every individual with human decency — no matter if they are starting their recovery journey or continuing. The path to recovery is not linear, and we meet each person where they are, providing consistent, judgment-free support at every stage.

Our staff are trained to respond with empathy, cultural sensitivity, and a deep understanding of the complex factors that contribute to substance use disorders.

Comprehensive Recovery Services
